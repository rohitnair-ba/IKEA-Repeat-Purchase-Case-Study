# 🛋️ IKEA India – Repeat Purchase Diagnosis

> A Business Analysis Case Study focused on understanding why a significant number of first-time IKEA India customers do not return for another purchase.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This case study analyzes IKEA India's repeat purchase challenge using a structured Business Analysis approach.

Rather than jumping directly to solutions, the study identifies multiple opportunity spaces, prioritizes them using the RICE framework, proposes a research plan, and clearly states assumptions before any implementation decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objective

- Understand the drivers behind low repeat purchases
- Identify candidate opportunity spaces
- Prioritize research efforts
- Design an unbiased research approach
- Avoid solution bias by validating assumptions first

---

## 🧩 Business Analysis Process

✅ Situation Assessment

✅ Data Review

✅ Candidate Opportunity Spaces

✅ Prioritization (RICE)

✅ Research Design

✅ Interview Framework

✅ Candidate Solutions

✅ Assumptions & Next Steps
