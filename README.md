# 🛋️ IKEA India – Repeat Purchase Diagnosis
./images/project-preview.png
> A Business Analysis case study exploring why a significant share of first-time IKEA India customers do not return for a second purchase and how to prioritize further research before proposing solutions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This case study applies structured Business Analysis and product thinking to investigate repeat purchase challenges at IKEA India.

Rather than jumping directly to solutions, the analysis focuses on:

- Understanding the business problem
- Evaluating available evidence
- Identifying candidate opportunity spaces
- Prioritizing research using the RICE framework
- Designing unbiased customer research
- Documenting assumptions before making recommendations

The objective is to build a structured decision-making process instead of making unsupported product or business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The case study investigates declining repeat purchases among first-time IKEA India customers and examines multiple possible explanations before selecting a research direction.

The analysis deliberately avoids assuming the root cause and instead proposes a research-first approach.

---

## 🧩 Case Study Workflow

- 📍 Understand the business situation
- 📊 Review available customer and operational signals
- 🔍 Identify candidate opportunity spaces
- 🎯 Prioritize opportunities using the RICE framework
- 🗣️ Design unbiased customer research
- 📝 Define interview methodology
- 💡 Present candidate solutions as hypotheses—not conclusions
- ✅ Document assumptions and next steps
